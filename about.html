01 / THE IDEA

Good food.
No great occasion
required.

Lebanese street food, right here in Hammersmith.

You’ll find Baba G at 8 Lyric Square. A place for wraps, boxes and mezze, with Lebanese flavour at the heart of the menu.

We’re here for the lunch break that needs a little life. The familiar favourite you’ve been thinking about all morning. Or the “let’s try something different” kind of day.

Our approach is simple: flavour first, and a meal that feels like yours.
